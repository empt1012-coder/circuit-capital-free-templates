# Circuit & Capital free templates

Free templates, checklists and worksheets from [Circuit & Capital](https://circuits.fit). The pages on circuits.fit are the canonical copies. This repository is a Markdown version you can copy, adapt and keep in your own files.

These materials are general guidance for small and mid-size teams. They are not legal, tax, insurance, audit or procurement advice.

Interactive calculators, and the original CSV, spreadsheet and PDF downloads, remain on the source pages.

Paid services and the newsletter signup pack are not included here.
